execution behind it. I am applying because I see this opportunity as a
place that values not just the \emph{what} and the \emph{why}, but the

having technical skills; it's about having the empathy to understand the
trade-offs engineers face and the grit to help solve them.

\textbf{MavMarket} to thousands of attendees or iterating on
\textbf{Split-Wiser} based on real-time feedback, my focus is always on
shortening the feedback loop and driving momentum. I thrive in
environments where cross-functional collaboration is built on trust,
transparency, and a shared obsession with the customer experience.

I am eager to bring this energy---a blend of strategic clarity and
roll-up-your-sleeves execution---to your team. I am looking for a role
that challenges me to be better every day, and where I can contribute to
a culture of high standards and deep ownership.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
