# Yuvraj K - Career Upskilling & Learning Path

## 🎯 Strategic Goal
To bridge the gap between **Hardware Engineering** and **Data-Driven Product Management**, making you a "T-Shaped" professional who can not only build physical products but also analyze their market performance and manage their lifecycle.

## 📊 1. Data Analytics & Visualization (Priority: High)
*For Product Analyst & Data Analyst Roles*
- [ ] **Advanced SQL**: Learn Window Functions, CTEs, and Stored Procedures. (Resource: Mode Analytics SQL Tutorial)
- [ ] **Python for Data**: Go beyond basics. Learn `scikit-learn` for basic predictive modeling (Regression, Classification) to add "Machine Learning" to your resume.
- [ ] **Power BI**: Learn Advanced DAX (Time Intelligence, calculate tables).

## 🛠 2. Product Management (Priority: Medium)
*For Product Manager & Product Development Roles*
- [ ] **Agile/Scrum**: Get a "CSM (Certified ScrumMaster)" or "PSM I" certification. It is highly valued.
- [ ] **Design Thinking**: Take a short course on UX/UI principles (Google UX Design Certificate is good).
- [ ] **Tools**: Learn **Figma** (for basic wireframing) and **Jira** (for backlog management - you can get a free Jira instance to practice).

## 🤖 3. Robotics & Automation (Priority: Niche)
*For Robotics & Mechatronics Roles*
- [ ] **ROS (Robot Operating System)**: Learn the basics of ROS 2. This is the industry standard for robotics software.
- [ ] **PLC Programming**: Basic familiarity with Ladder Logic (Allen Bradley/Siemens simulations).

## 🏭 4. Manufacturing (Priority: Niche)
*For Manufacturing & Operations Roles*
- [ ] **Lean Six Sigma**: Aim for a "Green Belt". It proves you can optimize processes numerically.
- [ ] **Supply Chain**: Learn basics of ERP systems (SAP/Oracle) conceptually.

## 📚 Recommended Certifications to Pursue
1.  **Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate** (Coursera) - Covers SQL, R/Python, Tableau.
2.  **Certified ScrumMaster (CSM)** - For PM roles.
3.  **Six Sigma Green Belt** - For Manufacturing/Ops roles.
