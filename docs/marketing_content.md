## 4. LinkedIn Post #4 (JD Keyword Architecture)
**Headline:** "Tailoring" a resume shouldn't mean blindly stuffing keywords. I built a logical data pipeline to do the exact opposite.

When you find a new Job Description, jamming its keywords into your resume breaks the logic of your existing bullet points. I built a workflow for my AI Agent to treat JD keywords not as buzzwords, but as strategic data points.

**The Logic (The Keyword Injection Pipeline):**
1.  **Extraction & Sorting:** The agent pulls all keywords from the JD and algorithmically categorizes them into two buckets: "Fits" (matches my actual career progression) and "Discards" (irrelevant or basics).
2.  **Safe Injection:** It takes the *Fits* and safely appends them to my YAML "Master Career Database", without breaking the carefully engineered metrics of my existing achievements.

**The Kicker (The Value Proposition):**
What happens to those discarded keywords? If they represent strong industry trends I don't know yet, the agent automatically appends them to my personal `learning_roadmap.md`. 

The logic here is twofold: The system ensures my current resumes are highly optimized for the role *today*, AND it automatically builds a customized curriculum for my long-term upskilling based on real market data *tomorrow*.

## 5. LinkedIn Post #5 (The Cover Letter Protocol)
**Headline:** Stop writing generic cover letters. I engineered a QA protocol to enforce narrative impact.

A resume lists what you did; a cover letter proves *how* you think. But most people just repeat their resume in paragraph form. To stop myself from doing this, I built a Cover Letter Audit Protocol into my Resume Agent.

**The Logic (Automated & Narrative Checks):**
My pipeline runs a two-phase check before any cover letter is generated:
1.  **Automated Formatting:** A Python script (`verify_resume.py`) parses the LaTeX source code. It rejects the build if it detects banned commands (like auto-dating `\today`), unfilled placeholders, or slight margin deviations. The presentation must be mathematically perfect.
2.  **The Narrative Audit:** The AI agent reviews the text against specific "Intangibles": 
    *   Does paragraph 2 highlight my *Builder Mindset* instead of just restating metrics? 
    *   Does it prove my technical empathy? 
    *   Is the letter completely company-agnostic so it scales perfectly across applications?

**The Value Proposition:**
By enforcing strict narrative rules, the agent prevents "fluff" and ensures I constantly communicate my overarching professional philosophy (e.g., bridging engineering with commercial success). 

I don't just automate document creation. I automate quality assurance for my own professional narrative.
