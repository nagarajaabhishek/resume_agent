# Line Efficiency & Real Estate Rules

## 1. Real Estate Optimization
Resume real estate is precious. Every line must work for you. Avoid "orphaned" words on a new line or lines that are less than 75% full.

## 2. The 115-120 Character Rule
- **Target Length:** A standard resume line (single column, 0.5in margins) holds approximately **115-120 characters**.
- **Execution:** 
    - **Single Line Bullet:** Aim for 90-115 characters to ensure it fills the line but doesn't spill.
    - **Two Line Bullet:** Aim for 190-230 characters to ensure the second line is at least **80% full**.

## 3. Strategy for Spillovers
If a bullet point spills over to a new line by only a few words (orphans):
1. **Condense (Cut):** Reword the sentence or use synonyms to fit it on exactly one line.
2. **Expand (Add):** Add specific technologies, context, or a secondary metric to fill the second line appropriately.

## 4. Bolding Strategy (The Scan Test)
Bolding helps the eye skip the "filler" words and focus on high-impact keywords.
1. **Bold Results:** Metrics and Percentages (e.g., **35% NPS increase**).
2. **Bold Technologies:** Frameworks and Tools (e.g., **LangGraph**, **AWS**).
3. **Bold Scale:** Numbers and Ranges (e.g., **5,000+ users**).
4. **Consistency:** Bold only the most critical parts; over-bolding reduces readability.

## 5. Substantial Impact Rule (2-Line Minimum)
- **Every bullet point SHOULD be at least two lines long.** 
- If a bullet is currently one line, expand the "[Z] By doing" portion with more technical detail, specific tools (e.g., Jira, Python, AWS), or a secondary impact metric.
- This ensures high real-estate utilization and allows space for both the action/result and the technical implementation details.
- **Target Length:** 180 - 240 characters total per bullet point.
