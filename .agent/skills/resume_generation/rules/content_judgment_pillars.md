# Content Judgment Pillars

> **Goal:** Evaluate resume data through the lens of a "Hiring Bar" to ensure every section (Experience, Skills, Tools, Methodologies) contributes to a winning argument.

## 1. Strategic Experience Argument
- **The Question:** Does this work history prove the candidate can do *this* specific job?
- **Positive Indicators:**
  - Achievements mirror the "Key Responsibilities" of the target JD.
  - Progression shows increasing complexity or leadership.
- **Red Flags:**
  - Generic task descriptions without impact.
  - Irrelevant technical details that distract from the role's primary goal.

## 2. Recruiter Empathy (The "Problem-Solver" Lens)
- **The Question:** Does this content address the recruiter's specific pain points for this role?
- **Positive Indicators:**
  - Use of industry-standard jargon that recruiters search for.
  - Clear mention of outcomes that recruiters care about (e.g., "Reduced time-to-market").
- **Red Flags:**
  - Purely academic or theoretical descriptions.
  - Lack of context on *why* a certain tool or method was used.

## 3. Hard Skill Depth & Recency
- **The Question:** Are the technical skills relevant, deep, and up-to-date?
- **Positive Indicators:**
  - Categorized hard skills (e.g., "AI/ML", "Cloud Infrastructure").
  - Skills are reinforced by mentions in the Experience bullet points.
- **Red Flags:**
  - "Keyword Stuffing" (listing skills with no proof of usage).
  - Outdated technologies that signal a lack of modern proficiency.

## 4. Tool & Application Mastery
- **The Question:** Does the candidate demonstrate proficiency in the specific "Stack" required?
- **Positive Indicators:**
  - Explicit mention of enterprise-scale tools (e.g., Jira, Salesforce, SAP, Figma).
  - Differentiation between "Expert" and "Intermediate" (if applicable).
- **Red Flags:**
  - Mentioning generic tools (e.g., "Microsoft Word", "Email") instead of professional-grade software.

## 5. Methodological Rigor
- **The Question:** Does the candidate follow recognized industry frameworks?
- **Positive Indicators:**
  - Correct application of methodology names (e.g., "Agile/Scrum", "SDLC", "RICE Prioritization").
  - Evidence of *how* the methodology was applied to drive results.
- **Red Flags:**
  - Vague references like "following team processes" without naming the framework.

## 6. Soft Skill Integration (Action-Based)
- **The Question:** Are soft skills *proven* through results, or just *claimed*?
- **Positive Indicators:**
  - "Led cross-functional teams" (Leadership).
  - "Translated technical requirements for stakeholders" (Communication).
- **Red Flags:**
  - Bulleted lists of soft skills (e.g., "Excellent communicator", "Team player") with no context.

## 7. Keyword Naturalism & ATS Optimization
- **The Question:** Is the resume optimized for machines without alienating humans?
- **Positive Indicators:**
  - Keywords are woven into the "Z" (Method) part of the XYZ formula.
  - Acronyms are defined on first use.
- **Red Flags:**
  - Awkward phrasing just to fit a keyword.
  - Invisible text or other "ATS hacks".

## 8. AI-Forwardness (Modern Productivity)
- **The Question:** Is the candidate using modern AI tools to multiply their impact?
- **Positive Indicators:**
  - Mention of using AI agents, LLMs, or automation tools to accelerate work.
  - Understanding of "Agentic Workflows" or "AI-assisted development".
- **Red Flags:**
  - Zero mention of modern AI/Automation in technical or operational roles in 2026.
