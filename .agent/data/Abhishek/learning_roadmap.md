# Abhishek's Learning Roadmap

*This living document tracks technical gaps, strategic concepts, and industry-specific tools identified through job description analysis. Prioritize learning these to continue elevating your profile for Senior TPM, PO, BA, and GTM roles.*

---

## 🏎️ High Priority: Technical & AI Infrastructure
These are highly sought-after in AI/ML and TPM roles but were identified as gaps or substitutions in recent analyses.

- **Vector Databases (Advanced)**: Deepen knowledge beyond NeonDB. Explore **Pinecone**, **Milvus**, or **Weaviate**, as these are the industry standards for major RAG architectures.
- **Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)**: **SAP** or **Oracle**. Even high-level familiarity with how they integrate with microservices is highly valuable for SaaS/Enterprise roles.
- **Data Engineering Pipelines**: Gain hands-on experience with **Apache Kafka** (event streaming) and **dbt** (data build tool) for modern data warehousing configurations.
- **Model Deployment**: Understand the architecture of **TorchServe** and **Triton Inference Server** for optimizing throughput and latency.

---
